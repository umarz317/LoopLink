LoopLink

CarPlay for Android car head units. Wired or wireless.

Download APK

Works with

  • Suzuki Swift
  • Toyota Corolla Altis
  • Honda City
  • Generic Android panels in other cars

Pakistani models with an aftermarket Android panel. The panel needs Android 9 or newer, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi Direct needs Android 10.

Install

  1. Install the APK on the head unit.
  2. Pair your iPhone with the car's Bluetooth.
  3. Open LoopLink and tap Connect phone, or Connect with USB.
